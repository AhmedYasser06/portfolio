★ Top 5 of 320 teams · ITC Egypt ADC 2026

  • Dual-model YOLOv8 detection running concurrently on RGB and thermal feeds, geometrically fused via calibrated similarity-transform alignment for higher-confidence threat detection than either sensor alone.
  • A novel NMSE-based threat-prioritization engine scoring every live track on distance, closing speed, confidence, and size — to decide which target to engage first.
